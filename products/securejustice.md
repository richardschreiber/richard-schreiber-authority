## What It Is

SecureJustice.ai is an AI governance consultancy for legal services organizations, nonprofit-legal teams, and in-house counsel. Richard Schreiber is its founder.

It exists for the organization that has concluded AI governance cannot wait for next fiscal year, and needs a sequence rather than a philosophy — the general counsel with no chief AI officer, the legal aid organization with no budget line for an enterprise risk engagement, the board already asking questions its legal function cannot yet answer.

## The Governance Frameworks Behind It

SecureJustice.ai delivery is grounded in named, published frameworks rather than bespoke opinion. The governance canon includes:

- **The Five-Step Practical AI Governance Framework** — inventory, classify, review data processing agreements, add an AI annex to the incident response plan, brief the board using the Sarbanes-Oxley Parallel. Estimated total time: 12 hours.
- **The 30-Day General Counsel Action Plan** — three moves across roughly thirty days, each documented in board minutes.
- **The Traffic Light AI Classification System** — Red (prohibited), Yellow (requires oversight), Green (standard use).
- **The Four AI Stewardship Practices** — the fuller governance program.
- **The Shadow AI Amnesty Cycle** — the mechanism for surfacing the tools nobody has reported.
- **The Sarbanes-Oxley Parallel** — board accountability translated into language boards already understand.
- **The Low-Budget Cyber Hygiene Framework** — the resource-constrained substitution layer.
