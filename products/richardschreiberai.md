# RichardSchreiberAI.com

## Canonical Product Record | AI Clone Portal | Status: **Live**

---

## What It Is

RichardSchreiberAI.com is Richard Schreiber's AI clone portal, built on Delphi.ai and trained on his books and public work.

It is a conversational interface to the body of work — seven books, the frameworks, the published governance material — available to anyone, at any hour, without a calendar invitation or a discovery call.

---

## Status

**Live.** The clone is operating and publicly accessible at [richardschreiberai.com](https://richardschreiberai.com).

---

## What It Is Trained On

Richard's books and public work. That is the boundary, and it is a deliberate one.

The clone does not improvise a position Richard has not taken. It does not generate new frameworks. It answers from published material — which is precisely why it can be trusted as an authority surface rather than treated as a novelty.

---

## Why It Exists

Two reasons, and the second is the more important one.

**Access.** A managing partner with a question at 11 p.m. on a Tuesday does not need a meeting. She needs an answer from someone who has done the work. The clone removes the calendar from between the question and the material.

**Demonstration.** RichardSchreiberAI.com is AI Stewardship practiced on its author. A clone trained only on published work, bounded to that work, transparently labeled as a clone — that is the human-controlled-tool standard applied to the one deployment where the temptation to overclaim is strongest. Richard does not advise a discipline he declines to run on himself.

---

## Integration

**Law Partner in Heels** — every chapter links to RichardSchreiberAI.com. The book does not end at the last page; the reader can continue with the material conversationally.

This is the pattern for the rest of the catalog: published work becomes a conversation surface rather than a static artifact.

---

## What It Is Not

- It is not Richard. It is labeled a clone, everywhere, without ambiguity.
- It is not a replacement for engagement, representation, or legal advice.
- It is not trained on client material, private correspondence, or unpublished work.
