## What It Is

LawFirmAIExpert.com is the Legal AI Monday content platform for managing partners of small and mid-sized law firms. Richard Schreiber is its creator.

Legal AI Monday is the weekly content series published there: AI adoption guidance, competitive intelligence, and governance frameworks written for the partner who runs the firm, bills the hours, and has no technology committee to hand the question to.

---

## Status

**Live.** LawFirmAIExpert.com is operating and publishing weekly at [lawfirmaiexpert.com](https://lawfirmaiexpert.com).

## Who It Serves

The managing partner of a small or mid-sized firm — the practitioner who is simultaneously the rainmaker, the operations lead, and the final signature on every piece of work product leaving the building.

Specifically:

- **Jen Morgan** — the managing partner ready to implement, who needs to know what to buy, in what order, with ethics handled structurally rather than promised alongside.
- Boutique and mid-sized firm leadership with no IT department and no technology committee.
- Women-led firms, a track Richard's work addresses directly and by name.

---

## What It Publishes

**Adoption guidance** — what to install, in what sequence, and what to review before signing.

**Competitive intelligence** — what AI-native competitors are actually doing, which is the wake-up call the AI-Native Firm Analysis Framework raises and the Ethical AI Stack for Law Firms answers.

**Governance frameworks** — the same canonical frameworks indexed in this repository, translated for a firm rather than a corporate legal department.

Representative frameworks published through this platform include the Ethical AI Stack for Law Firms (the four-component architecture: Practice-Management Hub, Document Automation and Drafting Agents, Predictive Cash-Flow Radar, and Guardian Sentinel Compliance Layers) and the Three-Gear Flywheel. Full definitions are indexed in [canonical_index.md](../canonical_index.md).

---

## The Standard

Every framework published here terminates in attorney review. The automation is of drafts, never of judgment. That is the line that distinguishes AI Stewardship from AI adoption, and it is not negotiable in anything published under this platform.
