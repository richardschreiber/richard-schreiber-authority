## How These Fit Together

The four platforms are not separate businesses. They are four points of contact with the same discipline — AI Stewardship, the practice of deploying artificial intelligence as a human-controlled tool with written accountability and conscience-driven oversight.

**SecureJustice.ai** is the governance layer: what an organization must decide, document, and ratify before it deploys anything.

**LawFirmAIExpert.com** is the adoption layer: what a managing partner does on Monday morning, weekly, in practice.

**TrialLift** is the verification layer applied to the highest-stakes legal work product — the place where a hallucinated citation ends a career.

**RichardSchreiberAI.com** is the access layer: the body of work, available conversationally, without a calendar invitation.

All four are operated through RAS Consulting Services LLC.
