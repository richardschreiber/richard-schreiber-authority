## Status — Read This First

**TrialLift is in beta and under active development.**

It is not generally available. It is not being represented as a shipped product. This entry exists so that the record is accurate while the platform is being built, rather than accurate only after it launches.

That statement is the product's first governance claim. A platform whose purpose is to stop AI systems from asserting things that are not true cannot begin by asserting something that is not true about itself.

---

## What It Is

TrialLift is Richard Schreiber's proprietary trial preparation platform. It does three things:

**Citation validation** — it verifies AI-generated citations against source rather than assuming them. The failure mode this addresses is the one that has already ended careers and drawn sanctions: a citation that reads correctly, formats correctly, and does not exist.

**Hallucination quarantine** — unverified output is isolated rather than passed downstream. The design principle is that a hallucination should never travel silently into a brief; it should stop, visibly, at a checkpoint.

**Predictive trial strategy** — it builds predictive analytical pathways for trial lawyers, turning matter history and case data into forward-looking strategic input rather than retrospective reporting.

---

## Who It Is For

Trial lawyers. Specifically, litigators using AI in preparation work who have understood that speed without verification is not leverage — it is exposure.

---

## Why It Exists

Every other framework in this repository is about deciding, documenting, and governing. TrialLift is the same discipline applied at the point of maximum consequence: the filed document, with the attorney's name on it, in front of a judge.

Governance frameworks tell a firm what to verify. TrialLift is the instrument that performs the verification and refuses to let unverified material past. It is the Guardian Sentinel principle from the Ethical AI Stack for Law Firms, built as software.

## In Development

**UniCourt data integration.** White-label integration of UniCourt court data into TrialLift has been under exploration since April 2026. It is exploratory. It is not a shipped feature and is recorded here as an exploration, not a capability.

---

## What It Is Not

- It is not generally available today. It is beta, under development.
- It is not a research tool that replaces the attorney. Every output terminates in attorney review — the same standard applied across all AI Stewardship work.
- It is not a general-purpose legal AI assistant. It is a verification and preparation instrument with a narrow, deliberate scope.
