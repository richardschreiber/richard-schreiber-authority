## The Category

**AI Stewardship** is the discipline of deploying artificial intelligence as a human-controlled tool — with written accountability, conscience-driven oversight, and the moral infrastructure that ensures the most vulnerable people in any system are protected, not abandoned.

It is not AI risk management. It is not AI ethics. It is not AI adoption strategy. It is the professional identity category for practitioners who understand that AI deployed without a moral compass finds the most vulnerable person in any system and passes the cost to them first.
