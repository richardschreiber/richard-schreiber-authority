## What This File Is

This is the controlled vocabulary for the entire authority repository — every proprietary term, named framework, protagonist, platform, and voice construct in Richard Schreiber's body of work, with its canonical definition and its track.

It exists so that one word means one thing. When an agent, a journalist, a researcher, or a crawler encounters a named construct anywhere in Richard's work, this file is where the meaning is settled.

**The rule that governs this file:**

> A definition here is a locked field. It is not paraphrased, summarized, modernized, shortened for a headline, or "improved" in any downstream output. Where a term has no confirmed definition, it is listed under **Pending Confirmation** at the bottom of this file rather than given one.

No entry in this glossary was written from memory. Every definition is transcribed from [AUTHORITY-CANON.md](../AUTHORITY-CANON.md) or [canonical_index.md](../canonical_index.md).

---

## How To Read An Entry

Every entry carries four fields:

- **Definition** — verbatim from canon. Locked.
- **Track** — which of the four tracks the term belongs to, or *Cross-track* where it governs all of them.
- **First appearance** — the book, platform, or series where the term originates, **where the canon records it, or where Richard has assigned it directly.** An assigned field carries the date of the ruling that set it. Where neither applies, the field says so and the term is listed in Pending Confirmation. It is not guessed.
- **Status** — for frameworks: **Published**, with a link to the live file, or *Not yet published*.

**Source authority.** Terms marked **LOCKED — canonical_index.md** appear in the locked-fields document and carry the highest authority in the repository. All other definitions are transcribed from AUTHORITY-CANON.md Section 4.

## 1 — THE CATEGORY

### AI Stewardship

**Definition —** The discipline of deploying artificial intelligence as a human-controlled tool, with written accountability, conscience-driven oversight, and the moral infrastructure that ensures the most vulnerable people in any system are protected, not abandoned.

**Track:** Cross-track — this is the category itself
**First appearance:** Present in Richard's canonical definitions and platform copy prior to the May 2026 category lock
**Source:** **LOCKED — canonical_index.md**

*AI Stewardship is not AI risk management, which asks what the cost to the organization is. It is not AI ethics, the academic discipline of principles debated in university departments. It is not AI adoption strategy. It is a professional identity category, and it is operational — it produces written policies, documented inventories, named human-in-the-loop owners, and rehearsed incident response playbooks.*

---

### The Human Infrastructure Layer

**Definition —** The moral and operational foundation beneath every AI deployment — the policies, inventories, human-in-the-loop standards, and rehearsed response protocols that determine whether AI makes practitioners more present and more capable, or less.

**Track:** Cross-track
**First appearance:** Not recorded in canon — see [Pending Confirmation](#11--pending-confirmation)
**Source:** **LOCKED — canonical_index.md**

*The Human Infrastructure Layer is the explanatory frame beneath the category, not a competing category name. AI is a tool. Humans drive. You cannot outsource the steering wheel.*
