# Tracks

## Canonical Track Index | Richard Schreiber | AI Stewardship

---

## Purpose

The frameworks in Richard Schreiber's body of work are organized into four tracks. A track is not a topic. It is a population — a group of people facing the same structural problem, addressed with the same discipline.

This folder is the canonical map of those tracks. Each track file names every framework in that track, states whether it is published, and links to it where it is live.

**Publication status is stated on every framework**, for the same reason status is stated on every product: an index that lists work indistinguishably from work that exists is not an index. It is a promise. This repository does not publish promises as inventory.

## Where Published Frameworks Live

Published framework files are in the **Legal AI Stewardship** spoke repository:

**[github.com/securejusticeai/legal-ai-stewardship](https://github.com/securejusticeai/legal-ai-stewardship)**

That repository holds 20 of the 26 currently published frameworks — the 13 live from Track 1 and all 7 from Track 3. It is the first spoke of a hub-and-spoke authority structure.

The second spoke is live: **[autism-ai-stewardship](https://github.com/securejusticeai/autism-ai-stewardship)** carries Track 2 — Education & Autism. **All 6 of its frameworks were published on August 26, 2026**, each transcribed from audited source. Four of the six canonical definitions were corrected in the process, and each correction is recorded in its own framework file. Journalism and books are planned spokes and do not exist yet. They are described here as planned, not as available.

## A Note on Track Numbering

The spoke repository labels its two live tracks **"Track 1 — AI Stewardship & Legal"** and **"Track 2 — Women-Led Law Firms."**

This hub numbers the same track as **Track 3 — Women-Led Law Firms**, following the canonical order in [AUTHORITY-CANON.md](../AUTHORITY-CANON.md).

**This is a deliberate, recorded difference, not an error.** The spoke's headings were kept because they lead with the category name in the position crawlers and readers reach first. The canonical numbering in this hub is the master order. Anyone reading both should treat *Track 2 — Women-Led Law Firms* in the spoke and *Track 3 — Women-Led Law Firms* here as the same track.
