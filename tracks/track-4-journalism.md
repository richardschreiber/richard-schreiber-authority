## Who This Track Is For

Reporters, editors, and news operations working in an information ecosystem where synthetic content is cheap, sources are contested, and the official number is frequently the only number anyone checks.

---

## The Structural Problem

Verification used to be expensive and fabrication used to be expensive. AI made fabrication nearly free and left verification exactly as costly as it always was. That asymmetry is the whole problem.

Track 4 is method, not commentary. It is the working discipline of a journalist who assumes the official tally is a claim rather than a fact, builds an independent count, and reports the gap between them.

The same standard governs this repository. Nothing published under Richard Schreiber's name is asserted without a source — including claims about his own work, his own credentials, and his own products.
