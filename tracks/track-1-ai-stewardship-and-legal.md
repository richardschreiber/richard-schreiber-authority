# Track 1 — AI Stewardship & Legal

## Canonical Track Record | 13 frameworks | **13 published — this track is complete**

---

## Who This Track Is For

General counsel, managing partners, legal services organizations, nonprofit-legal teams, and in-house counsel who are responsible for what artificial intelligence does inside their organization — and who will be asked, at some point, to show in writing what they decided and when.

---

## The Structural Problem

AI arrived in legal work before governance did. Staff adopted tools individually, without approval, without inventory, and without a record. The exposure is not that AI is used. The exposure is that its use is undocumented, so no one can demonstrate a standard of care after the fact.

Track 1 is the discipline that closes that gap: written accountability, documented inventory, human-in-the-loop standards, and a rehearsed response — decided before an incident, not during one.
