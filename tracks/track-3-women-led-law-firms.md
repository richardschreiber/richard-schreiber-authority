## Canonical Track Record | 7 frameworks | **7 published — complete**

---

## Who This Track Is For

Women who own or lead small and mid-sized law firms and are carrying two full workloads at once: running the business and practicing law, usually with a household running underneath both.

The named composite for this track is **Jen Morgan** — a 12-attorney managing partner navigating AI transition, family responsibilities, and competitive pressure from AI-native firms. She is fictional, she is used consistently across the work, and she is the test: *would Jen Morgan recognize herself in this?*

---

## The Structural Problem

The constraint in these firms is almost never ambition and almost never competence. It is leakage — time, money, physical and mental energy, and emotional bandwidth draining out of the business through gaps nobody has mapped.

Track 3 maps the leaks first, then closes them with automation that returns hours to the owner. The frameworks move in one direction: from scarcity to capacity, and from capacity to a firm that runs without its owner in every seat.

Automation here applies to drafts and process. It never applies to judgment. Every framework in this track terminates in attorney review.

## Canonical Definitions

**The Scarcity Scorecard** — a diagnostic tool for women law firm partners mapping four leak zones: Time, Money, Physical/Mental Energy, and Emotional Bandwidth, to identify where life and revenue are being silently drained.

**Jen Morgan** — the named fictional composite managing partner protagonist used throughout the Legal AI Monday series and *Law Partner in Heels*. Jen Morgan is a composite. She is never presented as a real client, and no real client's matter is ever attributed to her.
